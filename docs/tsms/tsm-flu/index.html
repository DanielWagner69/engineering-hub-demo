ClauseStatementRevision
5.1Every component installed in a fuel-wetted or fuel-vapour zone shall be electrically bonded to structure with a resistance of not more than 10 milliohm.1
5.4Fuel feed line installations shall be routed with no high point between a shut-off valve and the engine interface at which vapour can collect.1
6.2Hydraulic components shall be compatible with the hydraulic fluid specification of the project.1
7.4Pneumatic ducts above 200 degrees Celsius shall be lagged where they pass within 50 mm of a fluid line.1
