TSMs: Technical Standards Manuals

Permanent IDPG-FW-0000102OwnerFramework ownerVerification statusUnverified (generic framework content, not yet content-reviewed)Last reviewed2026-10-12SourceRequirements v23 (clauses quoted)

A TSM is a broad document holding many statements. It is tagged with Design Types, Disciplines and Systems, and reaches an FDPS when any one tag matches (OR). Over-tagging is preferred to under-tagging; the designer claims conformance only to the statements that apply.

6B.4 Technical Standards Manuals (TSMs) shall be a requirement data type: the governing design standards, built from lessons learned and governing body standards (for example those of civil or military airworthiness authorities).
6B.5 TSM inheritance. Each TSM shall be tagged with one or more of Design Type, Discipline and System. Each kind of tag is optional, but every TSM shall carry at least one tag. A TSM shall reach an FDPS if any one of its tags matches (tags are combined with OR, never AND): a Design Type tag matches when a Part or Assembly in the FDPS's population has that Design Type, including the modification Design Type of a modified bought-in Part (2B.10) and the Design Type Standard Part of catalogue items (1.10.1); a System tag matches when a Part Instance of the FDPS is homed in a Sub-system of that System; and a Discipline tag matches when the Discipline is listed on the Hub page of the FDPS's owning Design Team (the Design Team of its owning Design Sub-team). Matching is never against the Discipline register as a whole, because every Discipline applies to every design (4.2). Where there is doubt, a TSM shall be over-tagged rather than under-tagged. Each Design Team shall list its applicable Design Types and its applicable Disciplines on its Hub page. A Part in an FDPS whose Design Type is not on the owning Design Team's list shall be flagged. Every matching tag is a basis of the Compliance Statement (6B.6).
6B.11 TSM statements. A TSM is a broad document holding many statements (for example, bonding of fuel system components sits within a Fluid Systems TSM, alongside statements that apply only to hydraulic or pneumatic systems). The Compliance Statement of an FDPS against a TSM shall list the statements of that TSM that apply to the FDPS, and shall claim conformance to those statements only. Each claimed statement may list the Parts to which it applies; listing Parts is optional, and a statement with no Parts listed is claimed for the FDPS as a whole (for example a routing or clearance statement that applies to the installation rather than to a Part). Statements that do not apply need no record, and no non-applicability record is needed for a TSM that applies only in part. The evidence of a Compliant statement shall cover, at each effectivity in its range (6B.9), every listed Part present there for a statement that lists Parts, and the FDPS for a statement claimed for the FDPS as a whole.
