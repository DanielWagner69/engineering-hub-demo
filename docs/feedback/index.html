Framework gaps found while filling the Demo

Permanent IDFEEDBACKOwnerProject DVerification statusUnverified (generic framework content, not yet content-reviewed)Last reviewed2026-10-12

Gaps and findings recorded while filling the Demo; they feed the next framework version (plan 3.2).

R-16: Supports and Secures links into a Sub-structure FDPS

D5012001-801: Systems physically mounted on it through other FDPSs' Supports or Secures links: Fuel (PI-D-0004105, PI-D-0004115, PI-D-0004116), Secondary Structure (PI-D-0004105). Not prompted by its Sub-structure lists: none. Inherited but with no mounted item in the current population (over-inclusion, agreed at reviews as no physical interface): Skins, Doors & Panels, Electrical Power Generation & Distribution, Pneumatics, Flight Control, Vehicle Management, Hydraulics, Landing Gear, Environmental Control.

Finding. The Sub-structure lists alone prompted every System mounted on frames 14 to 15A (Fuel through the clamp bolts on the frame 15A boss and the bracket fasteners at frame 14; Secondary Structure is its own). Adding the home Systems of parts that Secure to its parts would add nothing here. Hydraulics, carried by the twin-pipe bracket at frame 15, is not detectable from links at all, because the bracket's fasteners secure the bracket, not the frame; only the list prompts it. Recommendation: confirm the Sub-structure list as the only route (over-inclusion accepted) and keep the per-FDPS R-16 evidence check as an advisory report.

R-19: scope of the example

Closed. FD-D-0000009 now carries frames 14, 15 and 15A, Requirements (inherited through Frames and Bulkheads, Primary Structure and Brackets and Supports), TSMs by Design Type, Compliance Statements with evidence, reviews A to C with snapshots, agreements, checklist responses and an Action, and a released rev A with four baselines. It passes every per-FDPS check, and 11 new negative tests on it are caught.

Other gaps

  • Annex A lists and Design Team lists remain placeholders in the framework; Project D replaces them for the two modelled Design Teams only.
  • The interface agreement for a System inherited only by over-inclusion has no 'No physical interface' position; reviewers record Agree with that reason. Consider a position value for it.
  • Effectivity selector, review template generator and baseline compare are Phase 4.
